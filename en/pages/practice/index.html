Problem-Solving Practice

From a problem catalog to evidence-backed, reviewable consulting proposals

Clarify the problem, analyze evidence and compare options to develop consulting recommendations for decisions and handoff. Agents, skills and tools support the work alongside human supervision, security and governance. Validate on a few bounded cases, then expand around actual needs.

Updated 2026-10-27 · Synthetic data / simulated management review · An evolving practice record

01

The consulting agent framework

Compose expertise around the problem while reusing a common investigation and review process. Use AI where it adds value; use calculations, rules or process improvements where they fit better.

Agents · Skills · Tools

Agents coordinate work, skills provide domain and consulting methods, and tools perform bounded data queries, calculations and checks.

Security · Governance

Approved access, evidence and version history, review gates and stop conditions. People retain accountability and decision authority.

How do capabilities develop?

All 99 problems have candidate capability mappings, with only part implemented. Logical roles do not necessarily mean independent models, and skill specifications are not validated capabilities. Each case still needs suitable data, methods and professional judgment.

02

From a problem to a consulting proposal

Define → investigate → verify → compare options → review → hand off.

Deliver a diagnosis, evidence, recommendation, roadmap, responsibilities, resource assumptions and acceptance criteria. When data is missing, identify assumptions, limits and the next investigation rather than presenting speculation as fact.

How do people and agents collaborate?

Agents prepare analysis and recommendations. Managers request evidence or changes, accept a bounded deliverable, reject or stop. Revisions require checks, and decisions bind to the reviewed version. Real accountable owners decide goals, budget, risks and implementation.

Current reviews are simulated by the same assistant, not user approval or independent expert review. Scope is consulting and adoption planning, not customer software development, deployment or operational changes.

03

Make recommendations reviewable, traceable and stoppable

Clients need more than an answer: how is their data used, where do conclusions come from, and who retains decision authority? The framework addresses these questions through bounded access, evidence checks and version records. Work can be questioned, returned or stopped; analysis acceptance does not authorize implementation.

Current controls and limits
  • The prototype checks evidence against its source version, retains analysis and review history, resumes saved cases, and blocks decisions applied to outdated work.
  • Demonstrations use synthetic data and simulated identities. Enterprise identity and access controls, independent expert review and real-user usability remain unvalidated.
  • This website presents deliverables; it does not run agents or carry out business actions.

2026-10-27 milestone: three existing capabilities in simple new contexts

Freeze capabilities, define expectations, then run similar contexts without deliberately making problems harder. Preserve failures and record repairs and retests separately.

  • CS02 shift tickets: direct reuse 2/2; escalation/expiry 2/2. Unresolved work cannot be accepted; stopping survives reopening.
  • E93 supplier reviews: direct reuse 2/2; missing-source stop 1/1. Opposition and unknowns remain visible; changed sources block old proposals.
  • CS07 new-product support: direct reuse 2/2; authorization/validity/ambiguity denials 4/4. Version and access limits remain effective.

Three synthetic contexts with related conditions, not thirteen independent transfer trials or thirteen newly solved problems. Correct stopping or denial is not resolution. Same-assistant authoring and review, Mock Lead and deterministic Workers do not establish autonomous agents or real business outcomes. No new engine capability or Planner; formal Problem X remains reserved. Case maturity and counts are unchanged.

What do the checks establish?

The prototype has automated checks for calculations, evidence versions and review records. These checks do not establish business results or replace real-user and expert review. A complete proposal and an operationally resolved problem remain different milestones.
