COL03Human × AI Collaboration

When AI hands work to a person halfway through, can the person actually take over?

Human escalation fails when the person receives only 'manual review required' without the history, evidence, uncertainty, or remaining options needed to act.

01

What's the problem?

Human escalation fails when the person receives only 'manual review required' without the history, evidence, uncertainty, or remaining options needed to act.

02

What do we need to know?

Capture agent history, evidence, reason for escalation, workflow state, decisions already made, available options, and the deadline for action.

Key inputs

Agent history, evidence, reason for escalation, workflow state, decision deadlines

Existing systems

Agent platform, workflow, ticketing

03

What does AI change?

AI can compress long execution histories into the context a person genuinely needs to continue the work.

04

How do we solve it?

Create a standard handoff package containing state, completed actions, key evidence, uncertainty, options, and a recommended next step.

Solution Core

Human-AI Handoff Design

05

What does AI do?

Prepare a concise, evidence-rich handoff instead of simply stopping.

06

What do people decide?

Handle ambiguity, exceptions, and high-risk judgment without repeating all prior work.

07

What improves?

Make escalation faster and more meaningful instead of restarting the case from scratch.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
