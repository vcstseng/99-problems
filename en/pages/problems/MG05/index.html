MG05Management

Why did we make that decision, what happened afterward, and what did we actually learn?

Organizations remember the decision but lose the assumptions, alternatives, confidence, and expected outcomes that made the decision reasonable at the time.

01

What's the problem?

Organizations remember the decision but lose the assumptions, alternatives, confidence, and expected outcomes that made the decision reasonable at the time.

02

What do we need to know?

Keep decision memos, alternatives, assumptions, expected outcomes, confidence ranges, actual KPIs, postmortems, and subsequent actions linked together.

Key inputs

Decision memos, meeting decisions, assumptions, alternatives, expected outcomes, confidence/ranges, actual KPIs/outcomes, postmortems, subsequent actions

Existing systems

DMS/wiki, collaboration, PPM, EPM/BI, decision logs

03

What does AI change?

AI can reconstruct decision context and compare expected versus actual outcomes across a large decision history, but lessons still require human interpretation.

04

How do we solve it?

Store decisions with their assumptions and expected outcomes, then review what actually happened and make the resulting learning retrievable for future decisions.

Solution Core

Decision Memory + Assumption/Outcome Lineage + Expected-vs-Actual Review + Learning Retrieval

05

What does AI do?

Link decision evidence, compare expectations with outcomes, and retrieve relevant prior decisions when similar choices arise.

06

What do people decide?

Judge what was genuinely learned, distinguish bad outcomes from bad decisions, and update future decision rules.

07

What improves?

Build institutional learning instead of repeatedly making decisions without remembering why previous ones succeeded or failed.
