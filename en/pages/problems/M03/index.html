M03Marketing

For each customer, what should we offer, through which channel, and when?

The next-best action depends on customer need, timing, channel, offer cost, and contact fatigue, so generic campaigns waste attention and margin.

01

What's the problem?

The next-best action depends on customer need, timing, channel, offer cost, and contact fatigue, so generic campaigns waste attention and margin.

02

What do we need to know?

Combine transactions, browsing, campaign response, product preference, coupon use, contact history, consent, and offer cost. The system must also know when not to contact someone.

Key inputs

Transactions, browsing, campaign response, product preference, coupons, contact history, consent/opt-out, offer cost

Existing systems

CRM, CDP, e-commerce/app analytics, marketing automation

03

What does AI change?

AI can estimate response and uplift across many customer-action combinations, while policy and consent constraints should remain explicit and deterministic.

04

How do we solve it?

Score candidate actions for each customer, apply eligibility and contact rules, and choose the action with the best expected value rather than the highest response probability alone.

Solution Core

Propensity / Uplift Modeling + Next-Best-Action + Constrained Decisioning

05

What does AI do?

Estimate likely incremental response by offer, channel, and timing and recommend the best eligible next action.

06

What do people decide?

Define customer treatment principles, commercial constraints, and which actions are never appropriate.

07

What improves?

Increase relevant engagement and value while reducing unnecessary messages, discounts, and contact fatigue.
