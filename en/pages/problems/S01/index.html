S01Sales

How much demand should we expect next?

Demand is hard to forecast accurately, so sales targets and resource plans can drift away from what the market will actually support.

01

What's the problem?

Demand is hard to forecast accurately, so sales targets and resource plans can drift away from what the market will actually support.

02

What do we need to know?

Start by connecting historical sales with the signals that can move demand: pipeline, inventory, promotions, seasonality, and external market conditions. The goal is to understand what is changing before deciding how much to sell or stock.

Key inputs

Historical sales, pipeline, inventory, promotion calendar, seasonality, external market signals

Existing systems

CRM, ERP, WMS, DWH/BI, external data sources

03

What does AI change?

Traditional forecasting works well for stable historical series. AI/ML adds value when many changing signals must be combined, when products are new or data is sparse, and when forecasts need to update continuously.

04

How do we solve it?

Build a demand forecast that combines internal history with external signals, then compare scenarios under explicit assumptions instead of treating a single forecast as certainty.

Solution Core

Demand Forecasting + External Signal Integration + Scenario Analysis

05

What does AI do?

Combine sales history, market signals, product factors, and external variables to update the forecast and surface scenario differences.

06

What do people decide?

Decide which market assumptions are credible, choose the commercial response, and allocate resources.

07

What improves?

Improve forecast accuracy, spot sales gaps earlier, and allocate inventory and commercial resources more effectively.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
