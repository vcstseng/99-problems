E96System Effects

If a company keeps citing an AI interpretation, can that interpretation eventually harden into 'historical fact'?

AI summaries and labels can be written back into systems, re-used as evidence, and repeatedly summarized until one interpretation appears to have many independent sources.

01

What's the problem?

AI summaries and labels can be written back into systems, re-used as evidence, and repeatedly summarized until one interpretation appears to have many independent sources.

02

What do we need to know?

Preserve raw conversations and primary documents alongside AI summaries, labels, reports, knowledge articles, and every transformation or citation step.

Key inputs

Raw conversations, primary documents, AI summaries, labels, CRM records, reports, knowledge articles, citation/transformation history

Existing systems

CRM, DMS, knowledge base, BI, RAG, data lineage

03

What does AI change?

LLMs are excellent at turning messy evidence into reusable records, which also makes recursive reinterpretation easy. Once the primary evidence is lost, AI cannot reconstruct the original truth.

04

How do we solve it?

Keep source-of-origin for every claim, label derived content clearly, and show when multiple records all descend from the same original interpretation.

Solution Core

Claim Lineage + Source-of-Origin Tracking + Derived-vs-Primary Evidence Controls

05

What does AI do?

Track claim lineage and distinguish primary evidence from derived or repeatedly transformed content.

06

What do people decide?

Define what can become an authoritative record and return to original evidence when a narrative has been amplified over time.

07

What improves?

Reduce the chance that repeated interpretation quietly becomes unquestioned institutional history.
