MG03Management

The numbers have turned red. What is actually happening inside execution?

Strategy dashboards show lagging outcomes but often fail to explain the decisions, blockers, actions, and customer issues causing execution drift.

01

What's the problem?

Strategy dashboards show lagging outcomes but often fail to explain the decisions, blockers, actions, and customer issues causing execution drift.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect strategic goals and KPIs with project updates, meeting decisions, action items, customer escalations, financial and operational indicators, and management commentary.

Key inputs

Strategic goals, OKRs/KPIs, financial/operating indicators, project updates, meeting decisions, action items, customer escalations, management commentary

Existing systems

EPM, BI/DWH, PPM, ERP, CRM, collaboration systems

03

What does AI change?

AI can synthesize unstructured execution signals across meetings and project systems and connect them to deteriorating metrics.

04

How do we solve it?

Map strategy to execution evidence, detect where commitments, decisions, or dependencies are drifting, and show the management exceptions behind the red metric.

Solution Core

Strategy-to-Execution Mapping + Unstructured Execution Signal Extraction + Management Exception Detection

05

What does AI do?

Extract execution signals and connect blockers or missed actions to strategic outcomes.

06

What do people decide?

Determine which issues are causal, resolve cross-functional blockers, and decide corrective action.

07

What improves?

Move from dashboard awareness to a clearer explanation of why execution is drifting.
