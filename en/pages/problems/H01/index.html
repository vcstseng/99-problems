H01HR

How many people, roles, and capabilities will the business need next?

Workforce plans often extrapolate headcount while strategy changes the actual mix of roles and capabilities required.

01

What's the problem?

Workforce plans often extrapolate headcount while strategy changes the actual mix of roles and capabilities required.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect headcount, skills, workload, attrition, recruiting lead time, business plans, product roadmaps, and external skill signals. Plan for capabilities, not just seats.

Key inputs

Headcount, organization, skills, workload, attrition, recruiting lead time, business plans, product/strategy documents, external skill signals

Existing systems

HRIS, ATS, LMS, workforce planning/EPM

03

What does AI change?

AI can infer changing skill demand from strategy and work signals, but workforce choices remain organizational and financial decisions.

04

How do we solve it?

Translate strategic scenarios into future capability demand, then model headcount and skill gaps under alternative business plans.

Solution Core

Future Capability / Skill Demand Modeling + Driver-Based Workforce Planning + Scenario Analysis

05

What does AI do?

Extract capability requirements, forecast demand by role/skill, and surface where supply will not meet future needs.

06

What do people decide?

Set strategic priorities, decide build-versus-buy choices, and own workforce commitments.

07

What improves?

Align hiring and development with the capabilities the business will actually need.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
