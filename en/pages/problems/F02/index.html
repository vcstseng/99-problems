F02Finance

Revenue is there. Where is the profit leaking out?

Topline growth can hide erosion from pricing, mix, discounts, returns, cost-to-serve, logistics, and channel economics.

01

What's the problem?

Topline growth can hide erosion from pricing, mix, discounts, returns, cost-to-serve, logistics, and channel economics.

02

What do we need to know?

Break P&L movement across price, volume, mix, customer, SKU, channel, discount, returns, COGS, logistics, and channel costs rather than treating margin decline as one number.

Key inputs

P&L, price/volume/mix, SKU, customer, channel, discounts, returns, COGS, logistics and channel costs

Existing systems

ERP, EPM, CRM, DWH/BI

03

What does AI change?

AI can scan multidimensional patterns and identify unusual combinations, while the core financial bridge and margin arithmetic should stay deterministic.

04

How do we solve it?

Build a transparent margin decomposition, then use anomaly analysis to locate where leakage is concentrated and which drivers changed.

Solution Core

Profit Leakage Detection + PVM Decomposition + Multidimensional Anomaly Analysis

05

What does AI do?

Find abnormal margin patterns across products, customers, channels, and transactions and rank likely sources of leakage.

06

What do people decide?

Validate business explanations and decide pricing, sourcing, service, or channel actions.

07

What improves?

Turn vague margin pressure into specific leakage sources that can be acted on.
