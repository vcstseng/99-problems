VER02Reliability & Verification

If AI calculates a number, is recalculating it once enough? What if the formula or definition was wrong?

A calculation can be arithmetically correct while using the wrong metric definition, data population, or business rule.

01

What's the problem?

A calculation can be arithmetically correct while using the wrong metric definition, data population, or business rule.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect metric definitions, semantic layers, source records, queries, formulas, control totals, and business constraints before checking arithmetic.

Key inputs

Metric definitions, semantic layer, structured data, source records, queries, calculation rules, formulas, control totals, business constraints

Existing systems

ERP, DWH, semantic layer, SQL, rule engine, calculator, data lineage

03

What does AI change?

The model can explain or generate calculations, but numeric verification should rely on deterministic recomputation plus reconciliation to the right semantic definition.

04

How do we solve it?

First confirm what the metric means and which data it should use, then recalculate deterministically and reconcile against independent totals or constraints.

Solution Core

Semantic Definition + Deterministic Recalculation + Reconciliation + Calculation Lineage

05

What does AI do?

Interpret the requested metric, identify possible definition mismatches, and assemble the calculation lineage.

06

What do people decide?

Own metric definitions and resolve ambiguous business semantics.

07

What improves?

Catch both math errors and the more dangerous case of calculating the wrong thing correctly.
