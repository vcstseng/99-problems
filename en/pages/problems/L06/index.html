L06Legal / Compliance

After the contract is signed, what exactly did we promise—and when is it due?

Obligations are buried across contracts, amendments, SOWs, SLAs, and order forms, so commitments can be missed after negotiation ends.

01

What's the problem?

Obligations are buried across contracts, amendments, SOWs, SLAs, and order forms, so commitments can be missed after negotiation ends.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect signed agreements, amendments, notices, deliverables, due dates, invoices, owners, and fulfillment evidence through clause lineage.

Key inputs

Contracts, SOW/SLA, amendments, order forms, obligation/notice dates, deliverables, invoices, fulfillment evidence

Existing systems

CLM, DMS, ERP, CRM, procurement, PPM/workflow, calendar

03

What does AI change?

AI can extract obligations and trace clause changes, while dates, reminders, and workflows should be managed by deterministic systems.

04

How do we solve it?

Extract each obligation, link it to the governing clause and amendment, assign an owner, and place trigger dates into operational workflow.

Solution Core

Contract Obligation Extraction + Clause/Amendment Lineage + Obligation Calendar + Owner/Workflow Mapping

05

What does AI do?

Identify obligations, deadlines, conditions, and affected owners from contract documents and keep their source lineage visible.

06

What do people decide?

Resolve ambiguous obligations, assign accountability, and approve exceptions or renegotiation.

07

What improves?

Reduce missed contractual commitments and make post-signature accountability visible.
