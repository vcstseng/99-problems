F05Finance

Every business case says it will pay off. Which assumptions are actually credible?

Investment proposals are built on assumptions that vary in quality, comparability, and optimism, making attractive financial outputs look more certain than they are.

01

What's the problem?

Investment proposals are built on assumptions that vary in quality, comparability, and optimism, making attractive financial outputs look more certain than they are.

02

What do we need to know?

Extract revenue, cost, timing, benefit, and adoption assumptions from business cases and compare them with historical projects, actual outcomes, external benchmarks, and relevant risk evidence.

Key inputs

Business cases, revenue/cost/timing/benefit assumptions, historical project forecasts and actuals, external benchmarks, risk data

Existing systems

EPM/FP&A, ERP, PPM, DWH, DMS

03

What does AI change?

AI can standardize assumptions across inconsistent documents and retrieve comparable evidence, but NPV/IRR and portfolio math should remain deterministic.

04

How do we solve it?

Turn narrative business cases into a comparable assumption set, benchmark those assumptions against reference classes, and flag optimism, weak evidence, or missing ranges.

Solution Core

Business Case Assumption Extraction + Reference Class Forecasting + Assumption Credibility Review

05

What does AI do?

Extract assumptions, find comparable projects, connect forecast versus actual evidence, and identify unsupported or unusually optimistic claims.

06

What do people decide?

Judge whether assumptions are acceptable, set hurdle rates and risk appetite, and make the investment decision.

07

What improves?

Improve the credibility and comparability of investment decisions before capital is committed.
