E94System Effects

If people would not make a decision themselves, why does it become acceptable once it is called a 'model recommendation'?

High-impact choices about people, customers, pricing, or suppliers can be disguised as technical outputs, laundering value judgments into seemingly objective model scores.

01

What's the problem?

High-impact choices about people, customers, pricing, or suppliers can be disguised as technical outputs, laundering value judgments into seemingly objective model scores.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect AI recommendations with decision criteria, policies, approvals, impact assessments, appeals, and actual outcomes to expose where value choices enter the process.

Key inputs

AI recommendations, decision criteria, policies, approval records, impact assessments, appeals, outcome data

Existing systems

HRIS, CRM, risk systems, workflow, GRC, decision logs

03

What does AI change?

Models can quantify evidence, but they cannot decide what is fair, acceptable, or worth sacrificing. Technical language can hide those choices rather than eliminate them.

04

How do we solve it?

Separate evidence, prediction, business rules, and value choices, then assign explicit human ownership to decisions that materially affect rights or people.

Solution Core

Value-Decision Classification + Explicit Decision Ownership + Governance Review

05

What does AI do?

Make the components of a recommendation visible and flag where policy or value judgments drive the result.

06

What do people decide?

Own the trade-offs, fairness choices, and consequences instead of delegating moral responsibility to a score.

07

What improves?

Keep high-impact value decisions visible, contestable, and accountable.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
