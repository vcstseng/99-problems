CS07Customer Service

A customer issue arrives. What is the correct answer or resolution?

Support must combine customer context, product/version, entitlement, policy, troubleshooting, and past cases to give the right answer—not just a plausible one.

01

What's the problem?

Support must combine customer context, product/version, entitlement, policy, troubleshooting, and past cases to give the right answer—not just a plausible one.

02

What do we need to know?

Use the conversation together with product/version, entitlement, SLA, warranty, policies, KB/SOP, prior resolutions, and service history.

Key inputs

Ticket/conversation, customer and product/version, entitlement/SLA/warranty, policy, KB/SOP, historical resolutions, service history

Existing systems

CRM, ticketing/CCaaS, KB/DMS, product/entitlement, billing/order systems

03

What does AI change?

RAG and case reasoning can assemble the relevant evidence, but entitlement, eligibility, and amounts should be validated by deterministic rules.

04

How do we solve it?

Understand the case, retrieve only applicable knowledge and prior resolutions, verify entitlement and policy, then recommend a resolution or abstain and escalate.

Solution Core

Case Understanding + Entitlement-Aware RAG + Policy/Procedure Reasoning + Resolution Recommendation + Abstention/Escalation

05

What does AI do?

Assemble case context, retrieve applicable evidence, propose the next resolution step, and signal when confidence or authority is insufficient.

06

What do people decide?

Handle ambiguity, exceptions, customer-impact judgment, and final high-risk decisions.

07

What improves?

Give customers more accurate, policy-consistent resolutions while reducing unsupported answers.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
