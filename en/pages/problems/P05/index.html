P05Procurement

Does this supplier, transaction, or shipment cross a compliance red line?

Compliance risk can hide across ownership structures, sanctions, certifications, origin, destination, and transaction patterns that no single system sees completely.

01

What's the problem?

Compliance risk can hide across ownership structures, sanctions, certifications, origin, destination, and transaction patterns that no single system sees completely.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect supplier master data, beneficial ownership, certifications, contracts, transactions, origin/destination, shipping routes, and authoritative compliance lists.

Key inputs

Supplier master, ownership/affiliates, certifications, contracts, transactions, origin/destination, logistics routes, compliance lists

Existing systems

SRM, ERP, TMS, GRC, external screening

03

What does AI change?

Entity resolution and anomaly detection can uncover hidden relationships, while final screening against authoritative rules should remain deterministic and auditable.

04

How do we solve it?

Resolve the real entities behind suppliers and transactions, run governed screening, and prioritize ambiguous or unusual cases for investigation.

Solution Core

Entity Resolution + Compliance Screening + Transaction/Flow Anomaly Detection + Risk Triage

05

What does AI do?

Link aliases and ownership relationships, detect unusual transaction or routing patterns, and assemble evidence for review.

06

What do people decide?

Interpret ambiguous matches, decide escalation, and own compliance decisions.

07

What improves?

Reduce missed risk without burying compliance teams in avoidable false positives.
