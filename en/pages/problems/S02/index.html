S02Sales

Sales time is limited. Which accounts should we go after first?

Sales teams cannot pursue every account with equal effort, but prioritization is often driven by incomplete information, habit, or whoever looks active right now.

01

What's the problem?

Sales teams cannot pursue every account with equal effort, but prioritization is often driven by incomplete information, habit, or whoever looks active right now.

02

What do we need to know?

Bring together account fit, past wins and losses, engagement history, web behavior, and intent signals. The important distinction is between an account that looks attractive and one that is actually showing buying intent now.

Key inputs

Company attributes, historical wins/losses, engagement history, web behavior, third-party intent signals where available

Existing systems

CRM, lead database, marketing automation, web analytics

03

What does AI change?

AI can combine fit and behavioral signals at scale, but a score should support prioritization rather than become an unexplained black-box sales order.

04

How do we solve it?

Rank accounts using both structural fit and current intent, then expose the evidence behind the ranking so sales can decide where to spend scarce attention.

Solution Core

Account Prioritization + Propensity Modeling + Fit/Intent Scoring

05

What does AI do?

Score account fit, detect buying signals, and surface the strongest evidence for why an account should move up or down the list.

06

What do people decide?

Set the target profile, challenge weak signals, and decide which accounts deserve active pursuit.

07

What improves?

Spend more selling time on accounts with stronger evidence of near-term value and reduce wasted outreach.
