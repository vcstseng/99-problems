S07Sales

Which deals are most likely to close, and will we hit the month?

Pipeline totals often overstate reality because opportunities differ in quality, timing, and evidence of buyer commitment.

01

What's the problem?

Pipeline totals often overstate reality because opportunities differ in quality, timing, and evidence of buyer commitment.

02

What do we need to know?

Combine stage, deal value, activity history, meeting and email signals, time spent in stage, and historical win/loss patterns. A forecast should reflect deal-specific risk rather than fixed stage percentages alone.

Key inputs

Deal stage, value, activity history, email/meeting signals, stage timestamps, historical wins/losses

Existing systems

CRM, sales engagement, calendar/email, BI

03

What does AI change?

AI can learn patterns behind deal progression and detect risk signals hidden in activity history, but forecast probabilities should remain calibrated and explainable enough for sales judgment.

04

How do we solve it?

Estimate deal-level win probability and timing, flag stalled or weakening opportunities, and roll those estimates into a more evidence-based pipeline forecast.

Solution Core

Win Probability Modeling + Pipeline Forecasting + Deal Risk Detection

05

What does AI do?

Score deal probability, identify risk signals, and update expected pipeline contribution as new evidence arrives.

06

What do people decide?

Challenge the model with account context, decide interventions, and own the submitted forecast.

07

What improves?

Improve forecast credibility and focus sales effort on deals where intervention can still change the result.
