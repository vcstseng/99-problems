F01Finance

At the current trajectory, where will the year end—and which assumptions have broken?

Annual plans become stale as volumes, prices, costs, pipeline, and operating drivers change, yet management often continues using outdated assumptions.

01

What's the problem?

Annual plans become stale as volumes, prices, costs, pipeline, and operating drivers change, yet management often continues using outdated assumptions.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect actuals, budget, forecast, orders, pipeline, cost drivers, pricing, prior forecasts, and external assumptions. Track not only forecast error, but which underlying assumptions stopped being true.

Key inputs

Actuals, budget, forecast, orders, pipeline, costs, prices, operating drivers, historical forecasts, external assumptions

Existing systems

ERP, EPM/FP&A, CRM, DWH/BI

03

What does AI change?

AI can detect changing driver relationships and recurring forecast bias, but financial logic and accounting definitions should remain explicit.

04

How do we solve it?

Run a driver-based rolling forecast, monitor assumption drift, and show management which changes explain the revised outlook.

Solution Core

Driver-Based Rolling Forecasting + Forecast Bias Detection + Assumption Monitoring

05

What does AI do?

Update forecast drivers from new evidence, detect bias, and flag assumptions that no longer match observed conditions.

06

What do people decide?

Approve key assumptions, decide management actions, and own the official forecast.

07

What improves?

Produce a more current outlook and surface problems while there is still time to respond.
