P01Procurement

A new product has no sales history. How much should we buy first?

Initial buy decisions must be made before direct demand history exists, creating a high risk of both stockouts and dead inventory.

01

What's the problem?

Initial buy decisions must be made before direct demand history exists, creating a high risk of both stockouts and dead inventory.

02

What do we need to know?

Use product attributes, images and descriptions, similar-product history, margin, seasonality, trends, lead time, and purchasing budget to estimate a reasonable starting range.

Key inputs

New-product attributes, images/descriptions, similar-product sales, margin, seasonality, trends, lead time, purchasing budget

Existing systems

ERP, POS, WMS, PIM, DWH/BI, external trend data

03

What does AI change?

Multimodal similarity and cold-start models can borrow evidence from comparable products, but uncertainty should remain visible rather than hidden behind one point forecast.

04

How do we solve it?

Find credible analogue products, estimate a demand range, and optimize the initial buy within margin, lead-time, and budget constraints.

Solution Core

Cold-Start Demand Estimation + Multimodal Product Similarity + Initial Buy Optimization

05

What does AI do?

Identify comparable products, estimate cold-start demand, and quantify uncertainty around the initial buy.

06

What do people decide?

Judge analogue quality, merchandising strategy, and acceptable inventory risk.

07

What improves?

Make first-buy decisions with more evidence and less exposure to immediate overstock or stockout.
