H07HR

When key people leave, how do we keep the know-how from leaving with them?

Critical knowledge often lives in experience, exceptions, and decision history rather than in formal SOPs, so turnover can erase how work is really done.

01

What's the problem?

Critical knowledge often lives in experience, exceptions, and decision history rather than in formal SOPs, so turnover can erase how work is really done.

02

What do we need to know?

Capture expert interviews, SOPs, cases, decisions, exceptions, tickets, and the situations where written process differs from real practice.

Key inputs

Expert interviews, SOPs, documents, cases, historical decisions, exceptions, tickets

Existing systems

DMS/SharePoint, knowledge base, wiki, ticketing/collaboration

03

What does AI change?

LLMs can accelerate extraction and structuring of tacit knowledge, but captured content still needs expert validation and provenance.

04

How do we solve it?

Run structured knowledge elicitation, convert experience into reusable cases and decision patterns, and make it retrievable with clear source links.

Solution Core

Structured Knowledge Elicitation + Knowledge Extraction + Knowledge Structuring + RAG

05

What does AI do?

Extract recurring concepts, exceptions, decision logic, and relevant examples from expert knowledge and operational records.

06

What do people decide?

Validate what is correct, distinguish rules from judgment, and identify the knowledge that truly matters.

07

What improves?

Reduce knowledge loss and make critical experience reusable after people move on.
