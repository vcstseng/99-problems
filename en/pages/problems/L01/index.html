L01Legal / Compliance

A regulation changed. Which parts apply to us, and what must the company change?

Regulatory changes are hard to translate into concrete impacts across products, policies, processes, controls, contracts, and owners.

01

What's the problem?

Regulatory changes are hard to translate into concrete impacts across products, policies, processes, controls, contracts, and owners.

02

What do we need to know?

Use versioned regulations and regulatory notices alongside internal policy, product/service inventory, process maps, control inventory, contracts, and ownership.

Key inputs

Regulation versions, regulatory notices, obligations/requirements, internal policies, product/service inventory, processes, controls, contracts, owners

Existing systems

GRC, DMS/legal KB, policy management

03

What does AI change?

AI can extract obligations and map them to internal artifacts quickly, but legal applicability must remain source-grounded and reviewable.

04

How do we solve it?

Detect what changed, extract the obligation, map it to affected internal processes and controls, and create an evidence-backed impact list for legal review.

Solution Core

Regulatory Change Detection + Obligation Extraction + Internal Impact Mapping + Source-Grounded RAG

05

What does AI do?

Compare regulatory versions, extract obligations, and find potentially affected internal policies, controls, products, and contracts.

06

What do people decide?

Interpret applicability, resolve ambiguity, and approve the required business changes.

07

What improves?

Shorten the path from regulatory change to a controlled, traceable implementation response.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
