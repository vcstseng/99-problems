L05Legal / Compliance

Risk signals are everywhere. With limited audit capacity, what should we audit first?

Annual audit plans can lag emerging risks because relevant signals are scattered across incidents, complaints, controls, regulation, management materials, and external events.

01

What's the problem?

Annual audit plans can lag emerging risks because relevant signals are scattered across incidents, complaints, controls, regulation, management materials, and external events.

02

What do we need to know?

Bring together the audit universe, control exceptions, incidents, complaints, board and management materials, regulatory changes, external events, findings, and remediation.

Key inputs

Audit universe, control exceptions, incidents, whistleblower/complaint signals, board/management materials, regulatory changes, external events, findings, remediation

Existing systems

GRC, audit management, incident/case systems, DMS

03

What does AI change?

AI can synthesize weak signals across diverse sources, but audit prioritization must remain tied to risk appetite and independent audit judgment.

04

How do we solve it?

Extract emerging risk themes, connect them to auditable entities and prior findings, and provide a transparent evidence base for annual and rolling audit prioritization.

Solution Core

Emerging Risk Signal Extraction + Risk Theme Synthesis + Evidence Aggregation + Audit Prioritization

05

What does AI do?

Aggregate risk signals, identify recurring themes, and rank areas where evidence suggests rising exposure.

06

What do people decide?

Exercise independent audit judgment and decide the final plan and scope.

07

What improves?

Direct scarce audit capacity toward risks that are becoming important before they are fully visible in lagging metrics.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
