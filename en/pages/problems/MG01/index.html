MG01Management

Are the assumptions behind our strategy still true?

Strategies are often approved once and then monitored through outcomes, while the assumptions that justified the strategy quietly change underneath them.

01

What's the problem?

Strategies are often approved once and then monitored through outcomes, while the assumptions that justified the strategy quietly change underneath them.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect strategy and decision documents with explicit assumptions, external research, market, competitor, technology and regulatory signals, plus internal financial and operating indicators.

Key inputs

Strategy/decision documents, key assumptions, external research, market/competitive/technology/regulatory signals, internal financial and operating indicators

Existing systems

DWH/BI, strategy docs, CRM/ERP, external research

03

What does AI change?

AI can continuously monitor evidence against explicit assumptions and detect drift across unstructured and structured sources.

04

How do we solve it?

Turn strategic assumptions into a monitored register, link each one to evidence, and flag when the evidence no longer supports the original belief.

Solution Core

Strategic Assumption Extraction + Evidence Monitoring + Assumption Drift Detection + Scenario Analysis

05

What does AI do?

Extract assumptions, watch relevant internal and external signals, and summarize evidence for or against them.

06

What do people decide?

Decide when an assumption change is material enough to revisit strategy.

07

What improves?

Challenge strategy earlier—before deteriorating assumptions show up only as missed results.
