H08HR

Can we handle this employee case this way, or would it violate law or policy?

HR cases sit at the intersection of labor law, company policy, contracts, precedent, and case-specific facts, so confident but unsupported answers are risky.

01

What's the problem?

HR cases sit at the intersection of labor law, company policy, contracts, precedent, and case-specific facts, so confident but unsupported answers are risky.

02

What do we need to know?

Use versioned regulations, company policies, employment contracts, official guidance, and anonymized prior cases, with dates and jurisdiction kept explicit.

Key inputs

Versioned labor regulations, company policies, employment contracts, official interpretations/guidance, anonymized historical cases

Existing systems

DMS/legal KB, HRIS, case management, policy repository

03

What does AI change?

Source-grounded retrieval can find relevant rules and precedents quickly, but high-impact HR judgments require escalation when facts or authority are uncertain.

04

How do we solve it?

Retrieve the governing sources, compare the current case with policy and prior cases, state uncertainty, and route ambiguous or high-risk cases to qualified human review.

Solution Core

Source-Grounded RAG + Policy/Regulation Reasoning + Case Retrieval + Human Escalation

05

What does AI do?

Find relevant rules, extract applicable conditions, retrieve comparable cases, and show source-backed reasoning.

06

What do people decide?

Interpret ambiguity, consider employee impact and legal risk, and make the final case decision.

07

What improves?

Speed up routine HR guidance while reducing unsupported or inconsistent high-risk decisions.
