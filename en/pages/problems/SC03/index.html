SC03Supply Chain

Quality is slipping. Which supplier, lot, or process caused it?

Defects emerge across supplier lots, material genealogy, process settings, and production history, making root cause hard to isolate.

01

What's the problem?

Defects emerge across supplier lots, material genealogy, process settings, and production history, making root cause hard to isolate.

02

What do we need to know?

Link supplier, material and production lots, incoming inspection, yield, process parameters, defects, returns, and failure reports through complete genealogy.

Key inputs

Supplier, material/production lot genealogy, incoming inspection, yield, process parameters, defects, RMA/DOA, failure reports

Existing systems

QMS, MES, ERP, PLM

03

What does AI change?

AI can rank multivariate patterns and interpret failure reports, but genealogy and quality facts must remain traceable.

04

How do we solve it?

Trace defects through lot genealogy, detect abnormal combinations, and rank plausible supplier or process causes for engineering validation.

Solution Core

Lot Genealogy Analysis + Quality Anomaly Detection + Multivariate Root-Cause Ranking + Failure Report Intelligence

05

What does AI do?

Find correlated lot/process patterns and extract evidence from unstructured failure reports.

06

What do people decide?

Confirm root cause with engineering evidence and decide containment or corrective action.

07

What improves?

Shorten quality investigations and contain the real source of failure faster.
