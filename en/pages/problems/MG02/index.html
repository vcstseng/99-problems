MG02Management

The direction is set, but people, money, and time are limited. Which projects get resources first?

Portfolio choices are distorted when projects use inconsistent assumptions, ignore dependencies, or compete for shared resources without a common decision model.

01

What's the problem?

Portfolio choices are distorted when projects use inconsistent assumptions, ignore dependencies, or compete for shared resources without a common decision model.

02

What do we need to know?

Compare business cases, expected value and cost, resource needs, dependencies, risk, timing, and strategic alignment across the portfolio.

Key inputs

Business cases, project value/cost, resource requirements, project documents, dependencies, risks, schedules, strategic alignment

Existing systems

PPM, EPM, ERP, HRIS

03

What does AI change?

AI can extract dependencies and normalize messy project evidence, while resource allocation itself should use explicit portfolio constraints and optimization.

04

How do we solve it?

Standardize project assumptions, calibrate them against reference classes, map dependencies, and optimize the portfolio under real resource and risk constraints.

Solution Core

Dependency & Risk Extraction + Reference-Class Calibration + Portfolio Optimization + Scenario Analysis

05

What does AI do?

Extract comparable project evidence, surface hidden dependencies and risks, and generate portfolio scenarios.

06

What do people decide?

Set strategic priorities, risk appetite, and the final trade-offs between projects.

07

What improves?

Allocate scarce resources to the portfolio that best supports strategy rather than the projects with the strongest presentations.
