E97System Effects

If every agent optimizes its own KPI, why can the company still get worse?

Separate agents can each make locally rational decisions while collectively shifting cost, risk, workload, or customer harm across functions.

01

What's the problem?

Separate agents can each make locally rational decisions while collectively shifting cost, risk, workload, or customer harm across functions.

02

What do we need to know?

Observe each agent's objective, policy, actions, dependencies, constraints, and the enterprise outcomes produced when they operate together.

Key inputs

Agent objectives, KPIs, policies, actions, cross-functional dependencies, enterprise outcomes, constraints

Existing systems

Agent platform, ERP, CRM, HRIS, SCM, EPM, DWH

03

What does AI change?

The new challenge is not multi-objective optimization in one model, but many autonomous systems changing each other's environment through independent action loops.

04

How do we solve it?

Evaluate agents at system level, simulate or replay interactions, and add cross-agent constraints where local optimization harms enterprise outcomes.

Solution Core

System-Level Objectives + Cross-Agent Constraint Management + Interaction Simulation + Enterprise Outcome Monitoring

05

What does AI do?

Trace interactions and identify where one agent's improvement creates a cost or risk elsewhere.

06

What do people decide?

Define enterprise-level trade-offs and constraints no individual function can set on its own.

07

What improves?

Catch 'every dashboard is green but the company is getting worse' before local optimization becomes systemic harm.
