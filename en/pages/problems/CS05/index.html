CS05Customer Service

Before the engineer leaves, can we predict the fault, the parts, and the repair time?

Field visits fail when technicians arrive without the right diagnosis, parts, or time estimate, causing repeat visits and poor customer experience.

01

What's the problem?

Field visits fail when technicians arrive without the right diagnosis, parts, or time estimate, causing repeat visits and poor customer experience.

02

What do we need to know?

Combine the customer's description, error codes, device configuration, asset history, prior work orders, failure causes, parts used, resolutions, and actual repair time.

Key inputs

Customer issue, error codes, equipment model/configuration, asset history, work orders, failure cause, parts used, resolution, actual duration

Existing systems

FSM, CMMS, CRM, IoT/device logs, parts/inventory

03

What does AI change?

AI can understand free-text work orders and retrieve similar cases, while parts and duration predictions can learn from historical repair patterns.

04

How do we solve it?

Pre-diagnose the likely failure, recommend likely parts, and estimate job duration before dispatch, with uncertainty shown when evidence is weak.

Solution Core

Work Order Understanding + Similar-Case Retrieval + Failure/Parts Prediction + Repair Duration Estimation

05

What does AI do?

Understand the case, retrieve similar repairs, predict likely failure and parts, and estimate repair time.

06

What do people decide?

Confirm diagnosis on site, handle unexpected conditions, and make the final repair decision.

07

What improves?

Increase first-time-fix rate, reduce repeat visits, and improve field-service planning.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
