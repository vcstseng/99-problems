E98System Effects

What if, instead of AI adapting to people, people start reshaping themselves to be easier for AI to understand?

When AI influences performance, prioritization, reporting, and resource allocation, people may change how they write, choose work, or behave simply to become more legible to the system.

01

What's the problem?

When AI influences performance, prioritization, reporting, and resource allocation, people may change how they write, choose work, or behave simply to become more legible to the system.

02

What do we need to know?

Compare work logs, report language, case mix, exception rates, employee feedback, off-system activity, and behavior before and after AI adoption.

Key inputs

AI usage, work logs, reports, CRM/ticket entries, KPI narratives, exception rates, case mix, employee feedback, pre/post adoption behavior

Existing systems

Workflow, CRM, ticketing, HRIS, performance systems, LLM logs

03

What does AI change?

AI does not merely observe work once its outputs affect rewards and decisions; it can reshape the behavior being measured.

04

How do we solve it?

Monitor behavioral distortion, protect work that is valuable but hard to represent, and keep channels for off-system signals and exceptions.

Solution Core

Behavioral Distortion Monitoring + Human-Centered Process Design + Off-System Signal Preservation

05

What does AI do?

Detect changes in language, case mix, and behavior that suggest people are adapting to what the system can easily recognize.

06

What do people decide?

Protect non-standard judgment, minority cases, and important work that does not fit machine-friendly categories.

07

What improves?

Preserve human-centered work even as AI measurement systems become more influential.
