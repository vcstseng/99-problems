SC01Supply Chain

Designs keep changing. Do the factory and suppliers all have the same latest version?

Engineering changes propagate across drawings, BOMs, specs, approvals, suppliers, and production documents, creating costly version mismatches.

01

What's the problem?

Engineering changes propagate across drawings, BOMs, specs, approvals, suppliers, and production documents, creating costly version mismatches.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect drawings, BOMs, specifications, sample comments, approvals, supplier communication, and production documents through a clear change lineage.

Key inputs

Drawings, BOM, specifications, sample comments, approvals, supplier communication, production documents

Existing systems

PLM/PDM, ERP, DMS, email/collaboration

03

What does AI change?

Document AI can extract change requirements and compare versions across messy documents, while official revision status should remain governed by source systems.

04

How do we solve it?

Extract what changed, trace where the old requirement still appears, and verify cross-document consistency before production proceeds.

Solution Core

Document Intelligence + Change Requirement Extraction + Cross-Document Consistency Checking + Traceability

05

What does AI do?

Compare revisions, identify conflicting requirements, and trace affected documents, suppliers, and downstream artifacts.

06

What do people decide?

Approve engineering changes and decide which conflicting source is authoritative.

07

What improves?

Reduce production errors caused by outdated or inconsistent design information.
