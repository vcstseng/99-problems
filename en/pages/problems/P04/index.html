P04Procurement

Which supplier or critical material could fail, and how exposed are we?

Supplier risk is hard to assess because external events matter only when they connect to specific sites, materials, BOM dependencies, and alternatives.

01

What's the problem?

Supplier risk is hard to assess because external events matter only when they connect to specific sites, materials, BOM dependencies, and alternatives.

02

What do we need to know?

Link supplier and site performance with quality, capacity, geography, ownership, end-of-life risk, BOM dependencies, and external disruption signals.

Key inputs

Lead time, quality, capacity, historical performance, supplier/site, BOM dependencies, EOL, geography, corporate relationships, external risk signals

Existing systems

SRM, ERP, QMS, PLM, external risk data

03

What does AI change?

AI is useful for entity resolution and external-risk monitoring, but risk only becomes actionable when it is mapped to actual material and product exposure.

04

How do we solve it?

Resolve supplier entities, map them to BOM exposure, monitor external risks, and identify realistic alternative sources before disruption occurs.

Solution Core

External Risk Intelligence + Supplier Entity Resolution + BOM Exposure Mapping + Alternative Source Discovery

05

What does AI do?

Connect external events to suppliers and sites, estimate exposure, and surface substitutes or second-source gaps.

06

What do people decide?

Set risk appetite, validate alternatives, and decide mitigation or sourcing changes.

07

What improves?

Move from generic supplier news to concrete exposure and earlier mitigation.
