S08Sales

If sales miss target, which part of the engine broke?

A revenue miss can come from traffic, conversion, price, sales cycle, mix, channel, or execution, but topline reporting does not reveal the real cause.

01

What's the problem?

A revenue miss can come from traffic, conversion, price, sales cycle, mix, channel, or execution, but topline reporting does not reveal the real cause.

02

What do we need to know?

Decompose the sales funnel and compare targets with actuals across stages, conversion rates, average price, cycle time, customer, product, channel, and campaign dimensions.

Key inputs

Sales KPIs, targets, pipeline stages and conversion, average selling price, sales cycle, product/customer/channel/campaign dimensions, actual bookings

Existing systems

CRM, ERP, DWH/BI, marketing analytics

03

What does AI change?

AI can detect unusual combinations and generate diagnostic hypotheses across many dimensions, while arithmetic decomposition should remain deterministic.

04

How do we solve it?

Start with a transparent funnel and revenue bridge, then use anomaly analysis to narrow down the segments and process steps that explain the miss.

Solution Core

Sales Funnel Decomposition + Anomaly Detection + Hypothesis Generation

05

What does AI do?

Find abnormal patterns across products, customers, channels, and stages and rank plausible causes for investigation.

06

What do people decide?

Validate causal explanations, identify operational realities the data misses, and decide corrective action.

07

What improves?

Move from 'sales are down' to a concrete diagnosis of where performance changed and what to fix.
