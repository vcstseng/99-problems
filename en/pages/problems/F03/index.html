F03Finance

Manufacturing cost is up. Is it material, yield, labor, or process?

Cost variance mixes multiple drivers, so teams can spend time attacking the wrong cause if they only see the total variance.

01

What's the problem?

Cost variance mixes multiple drivers, so teams can spend time attacking the wrong cause if they only see the total variance.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect standard and actual cost with BOM, purchase price, material usage, labor and machine time, lot, process settings, yield, and scrap.

Key inputs

Standard/actual cost, BOM, purchase price, material usage, labor/machine time, lot, process parameters, yield, scrap

Existing systems

ERP, MES, PLM, QMS

03

What does AI change?

AI is useful for ranking multivariate patterns and interactions after deterministic cost variance has identified where the gap sits.

04

How do we solve it?

First decompose the variance into measurable cost drivers, then use multivariate analysis to rank the operational conditions most associated with the abnormal cost.

Solution Core

Cost Variance Decomposition + Multivariate Pattern Analysis + Root-Cause Ranking

05

What does AI do?

Detect combinations of supplier, lot, machine, process, and yield signals that explain unusual variance.

06

What do people decide?

Confirm root causes on the floor and decide which process or sourcing changes to make.

07

What improves?

Shorten root-cause analysis and focus corrective action on the drivers that actually moved cost.
