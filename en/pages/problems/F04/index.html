F04Finance

Will we run short of cash, when, and what should we do about it?

Cash shortages emerge from timing mismatches across receivables, payables, payroll, debt, tax, and expected inflows, not just from accounting profit.

01

What's the problem?

Cash shortages emerge from timing mismatches across receivables, payables, payroll, debt, tax, and expected inflows, not just from accounting profit.

02

What do we need to know?

Use bank balances, AR/AP schedules, invoice due dates, customer payment behavior, payroll, taxes, debt, orders, and expected receipts to build a time-based cash view.

Key inputs

Bank balances, AR/AP, invoices and due dates, customer payment history, payroll, tax, debt, orders, expected receipts

Existing systems

ERP, treasury system, bank APIs, AR/AP systems

03

What does AI change?

AI can improve payment-timing estimates and identify changing collection behavior, while cash aggregation and treasury constraints should remain deterministic.

04

How do we solve it?

Forecast daily or weekly liquidity, model expected payment timing, and compare funding or payment scenarios before a shortfall occurs.

Solution Core

Days-to-Pay Prediction + Cash Flow Forecasting + Liquidity Scenario Analysis

05

What does AI do?

Predict payment timing, surface collection risk, and update liquidity scenarios as new cash events arrive.

06

What do people decide?

Set liquidity buffers and decide funding, payment, collection, or investment actions.

07

What improves?

See cash pressure earlier and make treasury decisions before liquidity becomes an emergency.
