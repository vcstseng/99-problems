M04Marketing

Which customers are behaving unusually, and when is it real churn risk?

A customer buying less than usual may be churning, seasonal, or simply between normal purchase cycles, so blunt inactivity rules create noise.

01

What's the problem?

A customer buying less than usual may be churning, seasonal, or simply between normal purchase cycles, so blunt inactivity rules create noise.

02

What do we need to know?

Use recency, frequency, purchase cycle, category behavior, browsing, engagement, support, and historical dormancy definitions to understand each customer's normal pattern.

Key inputs

Transaction frequency/recency, purchase cycles, categories, browsing, engagement, support, campaign response, historical churn/dormancy labels

Existing systems

CRM, CDP, e-commerce/app analytics, support systems

03

What does AI change?

Survival and behavioral models can distinguish expected inactivity from abnormal decline better than fixed thresholds, especially across customers with different purchase rhythms.

04

How do we solve it?

Model expected purchase timing, detect meaningful deviation from normal behavior, and separate temporary inactivity from rising churn risk.

Solution Core

Churn Risk Detection + Survival / Purchase-Cycle Modeling + Behavioral Early Warning

05

What does AI do?

Estimate individual purchase cadence and flag behavior that departs materially from the expected pattern.

06

What do people decide?

Define what counts as actionable churn risk and choose the appropriate retention response.

07

What improves?

Intervene earlier with genuinely at-risk customers while reducing false churn alarms.

How does the agent framework approach this problem?

Start with the decision and evidence needs, then combine consulting skills and approved tools under human review, security and governance. The framework defines the approach; this page alone does not establish implemented capability or proven results for this problem.
