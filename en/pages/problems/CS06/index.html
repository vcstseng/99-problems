CS06Customer Service

Which B2B customer relationships are showing risk, and what should the CSM notice now?

Renewal risk often appears first in weak signals across usage, tickets, stakeholders, billing, and conversations rather than in a single health score.

01

What's the problem?

Renewal risk often appears first in weak signals across usage, tickets, stakeholders, billing, and conversations rather than in a single health score.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect product usage, adoption, tickets, SLA, stakeholder changes, contracts, QBRs, meeting notes, CRM activity, billing, and renewal timing.

Key inputs

Product usage, feature adoption, tickets, SLA, stakeholders, contracts, QBR, email/meeting notes, CRM activity, billing, renewals

Existing systems

CRM, CS platform, product analytics, billing, collaboration systems

03

What does AI change?

AI can extract relationship signals from unstructured notes and combine them with product and service behavior, but it should show the evidence behind a risk signal.

04

How do we solve it?

Build an evidence-based account-health view that separates specific relationship, usage, service, and commercial risks instead of collapsing everything into one opaque score.

Solution Core

Relationship Signal Extraction + Account Health Monitoring + Renewal Risk Evidence

05

What does AI do?

Detect changing usage and relationship signals, summarize evidence, and flag accounts that deserve CSM attention.

06

What do people decide?

Interpret account politics and context, choose the intervention, and own the customer relationship.

07

What improves?

Act on deteriorating customer relationships earlier and make renewal risk more explainable.
