GOV06Governance

The company has an AI policy. How do we turn it into a boundary agents cannot cross?

Policy documents are often advisory text, while agents operate through APIs and tools that require machine-enforceable controls.

01

What's the problem?

Policy documents are often advisory text, while agents operate through APIs and tools that require machine-enforceable controls.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect policy language with business rules, authority boundaries, agent configuration, tool calls, decision logs, and exception history.

Key inputs

Policy documents, business rules, authority boundaries, agent configuration, tool calls, decision logs, exceptions

Existing systems

Policy repository, IAM, workflow, policy engine, agent runtime

03

What does AI change?

Some policy is interpretive, but hard constraints should be converted into testable and enforceable rules outside the model.

04

How do we solve it?

Separate hard policy from guidance, translate hard rules into policy-as-code, test them, and enforce them at runtime before actions execute.

Solution Core

Hard/Soft Policy Classification + Policy-as-Code + Policy Testing + Deterministic Runtime Enforcement

05

What does AI do?

Interpret soft guidance and explain policy context while remaining constrained by deterministic enforcement.

06

What do people decide?

Decide which policy statements are mandatory, how exceptions work, and who can change enforcement rules.

07

What improves?

Move AI governance from written intent to controls that actually constrain behavior.
