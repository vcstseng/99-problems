M01Marketing

With a limited marketing budget, where should the money go?

Marketing spend is spread across channels, campaigns, and audiences, but attribution alone does not show which spending actually creates incremental business value.

01

What's the problem?

Marketing spend is spread across channels, campaigns, and audiences, but attribution alone does not show which spending actually creates incremental business value.

02

What do we need to know?

Use spend, exposure, conversion, revenue, margin, and enough variation over time to observe response. Experiments or holdouts are especially valuable for separating true incrementality from correlated demand.

Key inputs

Channel and campaign spend, impressions, conversions, revenue, margin, experiments/holdouts where available

Existing systems

Ad platforms, web analytics, CRM/CDP, ERP, DWH

03

What does AI change?

AI can model complex response patterns and help optimize budget allocation, but causal measurement is still needed before treating attributed revenue as incremental impact.

04

How do we solve it?

Estimate incremental channel contribution, model diminishing returns, and allocate budget against the business objective rather than last-click credit.

Solution Core

Marketing Mix Modeling + Incrementality / Causal Measurement + Budget Optimization

05

What does AI do?

Model response curves, compare allocation scenarios, and surface where additional spend is likely to have the highest marginal value.

06

What do people decide?

Set the objective, constraints, acceptable uncertainty, and final budget allocation.

07

What improves?

Shift spend toward channels and audiences that create more incremental value, not just more attributed activity.
