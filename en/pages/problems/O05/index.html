O05Operations

There are exceptions everywhere. Which one actually needs attention now?

Operational teams face more alerts and exceptions than they can investigate, while severity labels do not always reflect real business impact.

01

What's the problem?

Operational teams face more alerts and exceptions than they can investigate, while severity labels do not always reflect real business impact.

02

What do we need to know?

Combine alerts and incidents with asset or process criticality, customer and order impact, historical outcomes, and available runbooks.

Key inputs

Alerts, exceptions, incidents, abnormal KPIs, historical outcomes, asset/process criticality, order/customer impact, runbooks

Existing systems

Monitoring, MES/ERP, ITSM, BI/operations dashboards

03

What does AI change?

AI can cluster related exceptions and retrieve similar incidents, but priority should be grounded in business impact and explicit criticality.

04

How do we solve it?

Group duplicate symptoms, score impact, attach relevant runbooks or prior cases, and route the highest-value exceptions first.

Solution Core

Exception Clustering + Business Impact Scoring + Incident/Runbook Retrieval + Priority Ranking

05

What does AI do?

Cluster related alerts, estimate likely impact, and retrieve the most relevant response evidence.

06

What do people decide?

Define criticality, override context-blind rankings, and decide action on high-impact cases.

07

What improves?

Reduce alert fatigue and put scarce operational attention on the exceptions that matter most.
