CS04Customer Service

Do these different-looking complaints actually come from the same product or process problem?

Related customer issues are often split across channels, labels, and wording, delaying recognition of a common underlying defect.

01

What's the problem?

Related customer issues are often split across channels, labels, and wording, delaying recognition of a common underlying defect.

02

What do we need to know?

Connect complaint text with product, SKU, version, resolution, returns, process, and time trends across channels.

Key inputs

Cross-channel complaint text, issue taxonomy, product/SKU/version, resolution, RMA, process, time trends

Existing systems

Ticketing, CRM, product analytics, QMS

03

What does AI change?

Semantic clustering can connect differently worded symptoms and detect emerging issue groups before taxonomy catches up.

04

How do we solve it?

Cluster similar issues, stabilize them into a usable taxonomy, and link recurring clusters to product, version, or process root causes.

Solution Core

Semantic Issue Clustering + Emerging Issue Detection + Stable Issue Taxonomy + Root-Cause Linking

05

What does AI do?

Group semantically related complaints and detect rapidly growing issue patterns.

06

What do people decide?

Validate whether clusters share a real cause and decide product or process action.

07

What improves?

Identify systemic customer issues sooner and reduce repeated one-off handling.
