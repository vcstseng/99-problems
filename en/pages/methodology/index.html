Methodology

How real work was turned into 99 business problems

We started with public job information to observe real work requirements, then converted them into business problems that can be discussed, decomposed, and evaluated for where AI may genuinely help. The same structure is used across business problems, AI risks and governance, and system effects.

Observe real work requirements from public job information.

03

04

Group the problems into three lenses and their domains.

05

Keep Current

Refine the set as markets, work, and technology change.

Every problem is broken down the same way

A common structure keeps the 99 problems comparable, extensible, and directly connected to each problem detail page.

01Problem IDA stable reference for every problem.
02Where does it happen?The business or AI domain where the problem appears.
03What are we really asking?State the real problem in plain business language.
04Why is it a problem?Make the context, constraints, and real bottleneck explicit.
05What data and systems matter?Identify the evidence and systems the company may already have.
06Is AI actually useful here?Compare with existing methods before claiming AI adds value.
07What is the core solution?Define the problem-solving approach without assuming AI must be the answer.
08What should AI do?Specify the part of the real workflow where AI adds leverage.
09What must people decide?Keep judgment, exceptions, and accountability where they belong.
10What should improve?Return to business outcomes instead of treating AI adoption itself as success.
